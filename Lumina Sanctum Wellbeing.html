Do you chase, or do you shut down?

Most couples fall into one of two roles. Neither is the problem. Both are old protection.

The one who chases

When distance appears, you reach harder: more questions, more talking, more need for reassurance. Underneath, you’re afraid of being left.

The one who shuts down

When emotions rise, you go quiet or pull away. Underneath, closeness can feel like pressure you can’t meet.
